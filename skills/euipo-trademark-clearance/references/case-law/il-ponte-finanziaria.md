# Il Ponte Finanziaria ("BAINBRIDGE") — C-234/06 P (13 September 2007)

**Court:** Court of Justice (appeal)
**Topic:** Family / series of marks · proof of use of enough marks to constitute a family

## Facts

Opposition to "BAINBRIDGE" based on a number of earlier "Bridge"-formative marks. The opponent argued that its marks formed a **family or series** sharing the "Bridge" element, raising the likelihood that the public would see the applied-for mark as another member.

## Holding / principle

1. **Family-of-marks can raise confusion.** Where there is a **family or series** of marks sharing a common element, the public may be led to believe a later mark sharing that element belongs to the same family — increasing the likelihood of confusion (para. 62–63).
2. **But it must be proven by use.** That heightened risk can be invoked **only if the opponent proves use of a sufficient number** of the marks capable of constituting a "family" (para. 64). A family cannot be asserted from registrations alone where the marks are not used.
3. The judgment also confirms strict treatment of **proof of genuine use** and that defensive/unused registrations cannot be leveraged to broaden protection.

## Application in a clearance analysis

- Relevant where the earlier rights are a **cluster of marks sharing a root** (e.g. several "AURORA-X" marks by one owner). Flag the possible family-of-marks argument as **raising** the risk — but note it depends on the owner actually **using** enough of them.
- Connects directly to the clearance limitation that the register shows registrations, **not use**: a family-of-marks risk is an **open factor** the attorney must investigate (is the family genuinely used?).

## Key paragraphs
- Para. 62–63 — family/series of marks can increase likelihood of confusion
- Para. 64 — requires proof of use of a sufficient number of marks
