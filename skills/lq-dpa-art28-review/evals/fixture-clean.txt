DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT (syn-001)


between Northwind Ltd ("Controller") and Solaris Data BV ("Processor"),

made pursuant to Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).


This Agreement governs the processing of Personal Data by the Processor on behalf of the Controller in connection with the Services.


1. Processing on Documented Instructions
The Processor shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller,
including with regard to transfers of Personal Data to a third country, unless required to do so by
Union or Member State law.

2. Confidentiality of Authorised Persons
The Processor shall ensure that persons authorised to process the Personal Data have committed
themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

3. Security of Processing (Art. 32)
The Processor shall implement the technical and organisational measures set out in Annex II to
ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, in accordance with Article 32.

4. Sub-processors
The Processor shall not engage another processor without prior specific or general written
authorisation of the Controller, and shall impose on any sub-processor the same data-protection
obligations as set out in this Agreement by way of contract.

5. Assistance with Data-Subject Rights
Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Processor shall assist the Controller by
appropriate technical and organisational measures for the fulfilment of the Controller's obligation
to respond to requests for exercising the data subject's rights under Chapter III.

6. Assistance with Arts. 32-36
The Processor shall assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with the obligations pursuant to
Articles 32 to 36, taking into account the nature of processing and information available to the
Processor.

7. Deletion or Return of Data
At the choice of the Controller, the Processor shall delete or return all Personal Data after the
end of the provision of Services and delete existing copies, unless storage is required by law.

8. Audits and Information
The Processor shall make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate
compliance with Article 28 and allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted
by the Controller or an auditor mandated by the Controller.

9. International Transfers
Any transfer of Personal Data outside the EEA shall be governed by the Standard Contractual Clauses
set out in Annex III or another lawful transfer mechanism under Chapter V.

10. Personal Data Breach Notification
The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay and in any event within 72 hours after
becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach.

11. Audit Frequency and Scope
The Controller may audit the Processor once per year and additionally for cause following a Personal
Data Breach, on reasonable notice.

12. Liability
Each party's liability under this Agreement is subject to the limitations in the Services Agreement,
provided such limitations do not exclude liability for breaches of data-protection law.

13. Data Return Format
On return, Personal Data shall be provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format
within 30 days.

14. Sub-processor List and Changes
Annex I lists current sub-processors. The Processor shall give 30 days' prior notice of any intended
change, during which the Controller may object.


Signed for and on behalf of the parties as of the Effective Date.
